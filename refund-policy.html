Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026 · Thy Word International Bible College · Balanga City, Bataan, Philippines

This policy explains what happens to fees you have paid if you withdraw from a subject or from the College, or if we cancel a class.

This page is a draft and must be confirmed by the College before it is published. The refund periods and percentages below follow the pattern commonly used by Philippine institutions, but they must be checked against what TWIBC actually does in practice and approved by the College. Publishing a refund schedule the College does not intend to honour is worse than publishing none at all.

1. Why this policy exists

Enrolment requires a down payment of 50% of your assessed fees, with the balance payable in instalments. Because you pay before or early in the semester, you are entitled to know in advance what happens to that money if your circumstances change.

2. If the College cancels a class or programme

If we cancel a subject or programme you have paid for, and we cannot offer you a suitable alternative that you accept, you receive a full refund of everything you paid for that subject, including registration and miscellaneous fees. This applies regardless of timing and is not affected by the schedule below.

3. If you withdraw

Refunds are calculated from the date the College office receives your written notice of withdrawal — by email to twibiblecollege@gmail.com — not from the date you stopped attending.

Proposed refund schedule by date of written withdrawal
Written notice receivedTuition refundedRegistration & miscellaneous
Before classes begin100%Refunded in full
Within the first week of classes90%Non-refundable
Within the second week80%Non-refundable
Within the third and fourth weeks50%Non-refundable
After the fourth weekNo refundNon-refundable

Registration and miscellaneous fees cover costs the College incurs when it registers you, which is why they are treated differently from tuition once classes have started.

4. Compassionate cases

If you must withdraw because of serious illness, bereavement, or another grave circumstance, write to the College office. The Director may approve a refund, a credit toward a future semester, or a deferral outside the schedule above. We would rather deal with you fairly than hold you to a table.

5. Credits instead of refunds

You may ask for the amount to be held as a credit toward a future semester instead of being refunded. A credit remains available for [period to be confirmed]. Choosing a credit is your decision, never a substitute we impose on you.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Email twibiblecollege@gmail.com with your full name, Student ID, the semester and subjects concerned, and the reason for withdrawing.
  2. Attach your proof of payment or invoice number.
  3. The College office will acknowledge your request within [number of days to be confirmed] working days.
  4. Approved refunds are paid by the same method you used to pay, wherever possible.

7. International students

Students in the Qatar, Europe and Canada online programmes are assessed at the international rates published on the Admissions page. The same schedule above applies. Where a refund must cross borders, any bank or remittance charge is deducted from the amount returned, and we will tell you the expected charge before proceeding.

8. If you disagree with a decision

Write to the College Director at twibiblecollege@gmail.com setting out why. Nothing in this policy removes your rights under Republic Act No. 7394, the Consumer Act of the Philippines, or under the consumer law of the country you live in.
