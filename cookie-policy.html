Your Privacy

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026 · Thy Word International Bible College · Balanga City, Bataan, Philippines

This page explains what this website stores on your device. The short version: we do not track you, we run no analytics, and we show no advertising.

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies at all. There is no Google Analytics on this site, no Meta Pixel, and no advertising network. Nothing on these pages builds a profile of you or follows you to other websites. That is why you are not asked to dismiss a cookie banner.

1. What we store, and why

Storage used by this website
WhatWherePurposeHow long
studentID and authToken Student Portal, browser session storage Keeps you logged in as you move between tabs of the portal. Without it you would have to re-enter your password on every click. Deleted when you close the browser tab, or when you log out

That is the entire list. These are strictly necessary for the portal to function, so they do not require your consent under the Data Privacy Act or under EU and UK cookie rules. They stay on your own device and are never used to track you.

2. Third-party content that can set cookies

Two pages embed a Google Map showing the campus location: the home page and the contact page.

Google can set cookies and receive your IP address when a map loads. Because we do not want that to happen without your say-so, the map does not load automatically. It appears as a still placeholder with a button, and nothing is requested from Google until you choose to load it. If you never press the button, Google receives nothing from your visit.

Other pages link out to Facebook, YouTube and Zoom. Those are ordinary links — nothing loads from them until you click, at which point that company’s own privacy policy applies.

3. Fonts

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. This means your browser requests the font files from Google, which involves your IP address. No cookie is set by Google Fonts, and the request carries no identifier for you.

4. Controlling storage yourself

You can clear or block site data in your browser settings at any time. If you block storage for this site, the public pages will work normally, but the Student Portal will not be able to keep you logged in.

5. Changes

If we ever introduce analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will update this page and ask for your consent first — before it loads, not after.
