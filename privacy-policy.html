This policy explains what personal information Thy Word International Bible College collects about you, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. It applies to this website, the Student Registration Form, the Student Portal and the Instructor Portal.

1. Who we are

2. What we collect

When you complete the Student Registration Form we collect:

  • Identity and contact: surname, first name, middle name, email address, home address, mobile number, telephone number, Facebook name.
  • Personal details: date of birth, sex, civil status, spouse’s name where applicable.
  • Emergency contact: the name and contact number of a person you nominate.
  • Church and ministry details: religious affiliation, church name and address, pastor’s name, your ministry in the church, whether you belong to the Assemblies of God, and the name of a recommending senior pastor.
  • Academic details: school last attended, campus, programme, whether you are a new or returning student, the subjects you enrol in, your grades and academic record.
  • Account details: your Student ID and your Student Portal password, which is stored only as an encrypted (hashed) value that cannot be read or reversed by anyone, including college staff.
  • Optional: a profile photograph, if you choose to upload one.
  • Financial: your assessment, payments, balance, and any proof-of-payment screenshot you send us.

If you use the contact form on this website, we collect your name, email address, the subject you select and your message. That form sends your message to us by email through EmailJS; it is not stored in a database.

3. Sensitive personal information

Under section 3(l) of the Data Privacy Act, information about your religious affiliation, age, civil status and sex is sensitive personal information and receives stronger protection than ordinary personal data.

We ask for your religious affiliation, church and ministry details because ministerial formation is the purpose of the College and because our programmes are delivered within the Pentecostal tradition of the Assemblies of God. We ask for your date of birth, sex and civil status for student records and pastoral care.

We rely on your separate, explicit consent to process this category of information. That is why the registration form asks you to tick a distinct box for it, rather than bundling it into a single agreement. You may refuse. If you would rather not disclose your religious affiliation, contact the College office and we will discuss what is genuinely required for enrolment.

5. Information about other people

The registration form asks for an emergency contact and for your pastor’s name. These are other people’s personal data. Please tell them you have given us their details and why. We use an emergency contact only to reach someone on your behalf in an emergency, and a pastor’s name only to verify a recommendation. If someone you named would like their details removed, they can write to us at twibiblecollege@gmail.com.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Google LLC — our student records, documents and email run on Google Workspace (Sheets, Drive, Gmail and Apps Script).
  • EmailJS — delivers messages sent through the website contact form.
  • Vercel Inc. — hosts this website. Vercel does not receive student records; it serves the public pages.
  • Your instructors and college staff — who see only what they need in order to teach and administer your programme.
  • Government authorities — where we are legally required to disclose information.

7. Where your data is stored

Our records are held in Google Workspace, on servers that may be located outside the Philippines, including in the United States and the European Union. Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

If you are studying with us from Qatar, Europe or Canada, your information is transferred to and processed in the Philippines, where the College is based, as well as on Google’s infrastructure. We take this transfer seriously and apply the same protections described in this policy wherever you are.

8. How long we keep it

  • Academic records (enrolment, grades, transcripts) are kept permanently, as is normal for an educational institution, so that we can verify your studies years later.
  • Financial records are kept for at least ten years, in line with Philippine tax and accounting requirements.
  • Contact-form messages are kept in the college mailbox and deleted when they are no longer needed.
  • Applications that do not result in enrolment are deleted within two years.

9. How we protect it

  • Portal passwords are stored only as salted, hashed values. Nobody, including college staff, can read your password.
  • Every request for student data is checked against a server-side session, so one student cannot retrieve another student’s record.
  • Certificates of Registration, invoices and proof-of-payment files are stored privately in Google Drive and are not accessible by public link.
  • Access to the student database is limited to authorised college staff.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by law, within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of it.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed; to object to processing; to access your data; to correct it; to have it erased or blocked; to be indemnified for damage caused by inaccurate or unlawfully obtained data; and to data portability.

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, Qatar or Canada, you may also have rights under your local law — including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place.

To exercise any of these rights, write to twibiblecollege@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can, and within thirty days.

11. How to complain

Please raise the matter with us first — most things are quickest to fix directly. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines, 5th Floor, Delegation Building, PICC Complex, Roxas Boulevard, Pasay City (privacy.gov.ph).

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Where a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell you directly.
